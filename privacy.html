Privacy policy

Alastor rclone is used only by its owner, to access his own Google Drive with rclone on his own computer.

  • Files and the access token stay on that computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is collected, stored, shared or sold by this app or its owner.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
